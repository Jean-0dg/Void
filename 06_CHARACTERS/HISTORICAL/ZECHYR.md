# Zechyr

## Summary
A fervent follower of Weaver who received the goddess's last vision at the moment of her death during the Great Fracture. That vision — an impression of something ancient, patient, and watching from the edge of the dark — became the founding legacy of the Sect of Zechyr, the secret organization that bears their name and has operated outside divine authority for centuries.

---

## What They Received

At the moment Weaver was consumed by the Demon, she sent one final vision to every blessed she had ever linked. Zechyr received it — but as one of Weaver's most fervent followers, the impression may have arrived with more clarity or more weight than it did for others.

The vision: *something old at the edge of the dark. Watching. Still there.*

This was Weaver pointing, in the only way she had left, toward Paradox — the oldest god, the one who had guided her to the realm's edge when she was nascent, and who had been watching from the distance ever since.
