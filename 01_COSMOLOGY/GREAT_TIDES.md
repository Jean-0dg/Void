# Great Tides

## Summary
Great Tides are localized eruptions of accumulated Chaos Energy within specific points of the Void. They are not explosions of the Void itself — the Void is infinite and indifferent. A Great Tide is a contained, violent convergence event at a single point. What it produces depends on the quantity of chaos accumulated and the type of reaction it undergoes.

## Details

### Nature
- Great Tides are **localized events** — small relative to the infinite Void, but incomprehensibly vast by any other measure.
- They occur every **600 to 1000 years** (human time).
- They are not an explosion outward. The dominant mechanism is a **violent implosion** — an inconceivable mass of Chaos Energy converging to a single point and coming to an abrupt, catastrophic stop.
- Analogy: imagine millions of trucks traveling at 1000 miles per hour all colliding at a single point simultaneously — not exploding outward, but stopping. All that kinetic force absorbed into that one place.

### The Mechanism: Implosion vs. Explosion
The outcome of a Great Tide is determined by **two variables**:
1. **Quantity of chaos accumulated** at the convergence point
2. **Type of reaction** — whether the energy solidifies (implosion) or purifies (explosion)

---

## Outcomes

### 1. Realm Formation *(most energy, implosion)*
The largest-scale outcome. Requires the greatest accumulation of Chaos Energy.

- The chaos energy converges violently and **solidifies** — it "calcifies" into stable, rigid structure rather than exploding outward.
- This calcified chaos becomes the **fabric of a realm** (world, universe).
- The realm's structure is made of stable Chaos Energy flowing in and out in an unfathomable, incomprehensible manner.
- At the **center point of impact** — where the shock is most violent — the chaos begins feeding on itself, purifying into **[[MAGIC_OVERVIEW|Essence]]**.
- The result: a world of calcified chaos with a heart of mixed Chaos and Essence.
- From this Essence-mixed core, **living beings** emerge, each carrying a **[[CHAOS_SEED]]** — a fragment of that mixed energy that humans call a soul.
- Realms typically last approximately **300 years** before dissolving back into the Void. Some fail earlier. Some persist longer.

### 2. God Formation *(~1/10th realm energy, explosive purification)*
Rarer than realm formation. Requires significantly less accumulated energy than a realm-forming Tide.

- Because the accumulated energy is smaller, it does not achieve the mass required to solidify.
- Instead, the convergence **explodes** — the chaos feeds on itself aggressively, purifying vast quantities into **Essence** at extreme speed.
- The sheer concentration of Essence produced at this scale becomes **self-aware**.
- The result: a **god** — a conscious sea of pure Essence.
- More than one god can be born from a single Great Tide, though this has **never been recorded in history**.

### 3. Abomination Formation *(failed purification)*
Occurs when the purification process initiates but fails to sustain itself.

- The chaos begins feeding on itself toward purification, but the process is not strong or sustained enough to purify most of the accumulated chaos.
- The result: an **[[ABOMINATIONS|Abomination]]** — a lesser, partially-conscious entity made predominantly of Chaos.
- Described as a "mid-conscious lake of chaos." Not fully a god, not inert matter.

### 4. Demon Descents *(tear formation)*
- Some Great Tides do not produce a new entity or realm.
- Instead, the energy tears **through existing realms**, creating rifts between them.
- These rifts allow inhabitants of other realms to invade.
- Humans called these events **[[DEMON_DESCENTS|Demon Descents]]** and classified most otherworldly inhabitants they encountered as **demons**.

### 5. Essence Permeation *(diffuse saturation)*
- Some Great Tides dissipate without a concentrated reaction.
- Instead, the energy **permeates existing realms** with additional stable Chaos, making it easier for those realms to produce Essence naturally.
- These events tend to coincide with the birth of exceptional, prominent figures in the affected realms.
