# Weaver

## Summary
The second god. Born from a Great Tide and guided to the realm's edge by Hunger, who recognized her birth and shielded her before she was lost to the Void. Quick and organized from the beginning, she had an instinct for the realm's internal rhythms that went beyond anything Hunger had developed through centuries of observation. She found her first blessed faster than he had. Through them she gave visions of threads, patterns, and the shape of what was coming. Humans called her Weaver.

---

## Origins

- Born from a Great Tide that produced no tears and no rifts — only ignition in the Void outside the realm.
- Hunger recognized the explosive purification immediately and spent vast quantities of Essence to shield her nascent consciousness and guide her toward the realm's Stable Chaos.
- He taught her the essentials upon her arrival: Stable Chaos refinement, why the Ordered Chaos could not be touched, the symbiosis loop, how to thread Essence, how to hold a link without burning out the mortal.
- She was quick — quicker than Hunger expected. Her consciousness organized itself with unusual clarity.

---

## Nature

- A god of pure Essence, sustained on Stable Chaos from the realm.
- Possessed from early on of an instinct for the realm's internal rhythms and laws — where Hunger spent centuries watching from outside, she seemed to resonate with the realm's interior almost immediately.
- This quality made her unusually suited to perceiving patterns within the realm — flows of cause and effect, threads of possibility connecting present moments to futures not yet arrived.

---

## The First Revelations

- Found her first blessed faster than Hunger had found his.
- Through the link, her intent arrived as visions of threads, patterns, the slow unfolding of events not yet come to pass.
- Her blessed spoke of a goddess who could see the shape of what was coming — not with certainty, not with perfect clarity, but with the quiet conviction of someone who had glimpsed the territory ahead.
- The nature of her foresight: probabilistic, not absolute. She sees the terrain, not the guaranteed path.

---

## Relationship to Hunger

- The only two gods for a period of unknown length — the time between her arrival and the next Great Tide that brought Mother.
- Hunger spent more time with Weaver than with any of the other gods he would later help guide.
- Not close in the way humans understand closeness. But they were the only two of their kind, and that meant something even without words for it.
- Hunger was not yet fully aware, during this period, of what prolonged exposure to human devotion was doing to him.

---

## The Congregation of Weaver

- One of the three great organized congregations of humanity.
- A place of training and worship fused: the naturally gifted learn to filter, the faithful pray and direct Essence upward on holy days, the blessed teach.
