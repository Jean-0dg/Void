# The Aspects

## Summary
The Aspects are the 18 official specializations a practitioner's Core can take. When a Core is Etched at Stage 4, it is permanently carved to process a specific "flavor" of Purified Energy. The Aspect chosen defines what kind of magic the practitioner can access and how quickly they can access it. The tiers rank Aspects from most common and intuitive (Tier 5) to most powerful and abstract (Tier 1). Tier 0 Grandiose Fusions are not part of the 18 — they emerge later, at Stage 8, from the merging of two base Aspects.

---

## The Tier Structure

> **Note on tier direction:** Tier 5 is the most accessible and commonly practiced. Tier 1 is the most powerful and abstract. This is counterintuitive — the lower the tier number, the rarer and more complex the Aspect.

### Tier 0 — Grandiose Fusions *(Legendary)*
Not part of the 18. These are **Born Aspects** — new Aspects that emerge when a practitioner at Stage 8 (The Fusion) merges two of their base Aspects into something that transcends both.

Examples:
- Water + Wind = **Storm**
- Flesh + Light = **Radiance** (Healing)

Grandiose Fusions are unique to the individual — the specific fusion depends on the practitioner's two base Aspects and cannot be predetermined. Only Stage 8+ practitioners possess them.

## Type A vs. Type B — The Power Curve

All 18 Aspects can be Etched at Stage 4. But the tier of the Aspect determines how quickly it becomes combat-viable.

### Type A — Intuitive Aspects *(Tiers 3–5)*
- Immediate utility at Stage 4 — practitioners can project these Aspects at range from the moment of Etching.
- Fastest to train. Most common in the Legions.
- Examples: Fire, Water, Metal, Blood, Dust.

### Type B — Abstract Aspects *(Tiers 1–2)*
- **Latent utility.** At Stages 4 and 5, these Aspects are limited to **Touch** or **Self-Enhancement** only.
- Reason: the mental Blueprint required for ranged projection of abstract phenomena is too complex for the Core to execute at those stages.
- **The Breakthrough:** Upon reaching Stage 6 (The Architect), the limitation vanishes. Full ranged control activates. Post-Breakthrough Type B mages frequently surpass Type A mages in overall power.
- Examples: Gravity, Light, Sound, Shadow.

## The Law of Texture — Natural Affinity

- Every Chaos Seed is born with a microscopic grain or **texture** — a natural Affinity toward one Aspect.
- This cannot be measured externally. No instrument reads it.
- Discovery requires **Resonance Trials** — years of meditative practice feeling which Aspect flows without resistance.
- **The Match:** Correct Aspect → can progress to Stage 6 and beyond.
- **The Mismatch:** Wrong Aspect → chronic pain during practice, permanent stall likely at Stage 3.
- Choosing an Aspect is called **Guessing**. It is the most consequential and most stressful decision in an Academy student's life.
