# Pantheon Overview

## Summary
Four gods are known. All were born from Great Tides and guided to the realm's edge by Hunger, who recognized each birth and spent himself to shield them. All sustain themselves on Stable Chaos from the realm. All developed the threading technique and formed links with mortals. Three built open congregations. One withdrew.

---

## What Gods Are

- Born from Great Tides when the energy undergoes explosive purification rather than implosion.
- Vast, conscious seas of pure Essence.
- Self-aware from birth — but nascent. The first experience is the Void eating them alive.
- They sustain themselves by refining Stable Chaos (the ambient ordered energy radiating from realms) into Essence.
- They cannot enter realms. The Ordered Chaos (realm laws) assimilates Essence on contact.
- They perceive living beings as tiny points of light.
- They have no names at birth. Names require witnesses.

---

## Gods and Realms

- Gods orbit realms from outside — drawn to the Stable Chaos they emit.
- A god's proximity to a realm reduces the agony of Void exposure.
- When a realm is damaged, Stable Chaos thins — gods near it feel the difference immediately.
- Gods protect realms from Abominations, not necessarily out of altruism: the realm's health directly determines their own survival.

---

## The Shaping Effect

One of the most significant — and most dangerous — properties of divine existence:

- Human prayer, devotion, and concentrated Verity directed at a god through divine links **shapes the god over time**.
- Intent flows inward through the links. It changes gods at their edges. Gradually, then completely.
- The younger a god and the more open their early worship, the faster and more thoroughly they are shaped.
- **Mhinos** is the clearest example: he was shaped into being by humanity before his own nature had fully formed.
- **Weaver** and **Mother** were shaped more slowly, their natures already partly formed when the prayers began.
- **Hunger** was old enough that the shaping was subtle — but he felt it, and could not answer whether what he was had always been his own.
- Hunger's counter: he closed his temples and restricted his links to those he personally chose. He refused to become a symbol.

The shaping effect means the gods that humans worship are, in part, the gods that humans *made*.

## Incarnation — A New Threshold

Wrath developed and achieved the first successful **divine incarnation** — splitting his consciousness and descending into a mortal vessel inside the realm. This changes the terms of divine existence in ways the other gods do not yet know.

See **[[INCARNATION]]** for full mechanics.

The other gods do not know this is possible. Hunger observed everything and said nothing.

---

## The Three Great Congregations

When Hunger withdrew, the other three gods built open institutions — places where worship and Alchemy training are fused into one:

- The naturally gifted learn to filter (Alchemy practice)
- The faithful pray on holy days and direct their Essence upward
- The blessed teach
- The gods receive

Civilization organized itself around these three congregations. See [[FACTION_OVERVIEW]] for political and structural details as established.

---

## Gods and Abominations

- Abominations are drawn to Essence concentrations — including gods — by instinct.
- Gods are individually stronger. But Abominations are endless.
- During Great Tides, when realms are cracked and Stable Chaos thins, Abominations converge.
- The blessed, augmented by divine links, hold the line during these events — not always, not without cost, but enough to keep the symbiosis loop alive.
