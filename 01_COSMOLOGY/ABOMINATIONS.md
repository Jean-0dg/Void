# Abominations

## Summary
Abominations are entities born from failed Great Tides — specifically, when the chaos-to-Essence purification process initiates but does not sustain itself. They are lesser analogues of gods: partially conscious, made predominantly of Chaos Energy rather than pure Essence.

## Details

- Born when a Great Tide begins the explosive purification process (the god-formation pathway) but fails to complete it.
- The purification is not strong or sustained enough to convert most of the accumulated chaos into Essence.
- The result is a **mid-conscious lake of chaos** — aware to some degree, but not the vast conscious sea of Essence that constitutes a god.
- They are **made of Chaos**, not Essence.
- Described from a god's perspective as: *"massive churning things made of Chaos that had briefly dreamed of being something more and failed."*

### Behavior in the Void
- Wander the Void on **instinct**, not conscious direction.
- Drawn toward any concentration of Essence — gods, realms, anything that has structure — the way a current is drawn toward a drain.
- Pay little attention to a newly born, nascent god. Their draw toward Essence scales with its concentration.
- Converge when a realm is weakened — its thinned Stable Chaos output signals opportunity.
- They are individually weaker than gods. But they are **endless**.
