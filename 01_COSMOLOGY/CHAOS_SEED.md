# Chaos Seed

## Summary
A Chaos Seed is a specialized physical structure made of Ordered Chaos, located near the solar plexus of every living being. In common human terms, it is called a **soul**. It acts as a filter — drawing in Ambient Chaos from the realm and converting it into Essence. The passive output of this process (Stale Essence) feeds back into the realm's sustenance loop. In alchemists, deliberate cultivation builds structures within and around the Seed — first a Nexus, then a Form — transforming it from a passive filter into an engine of power.

---

## Details

### Nature
- All living beings born within a realm carry a Chaos Seed.
- The Chaos Seed is a physical structure made entirely of **Ordered Chaos** — the same compressed, law-governed state that constitutes the realm itself, but organized into a distinct specialized organ embedded within the body's biological matter.
- Its Ordered Chaos composition is what allows it to exist at the interface between the human body and Ambient Chaos in a way no other organ can — absorbing and processing energy that would damage ordinary tissue.
- Originates from the Essence-mixed core at the heart of a realm-forming Great Tide — where chaos purifies into Essence at the center point of impact.
- Some Seeds burn brighter than others naturally. This is innate, not achieved.
- Every Seed carries a microscopic **texture** — a natural Affinity toward a particular alchemical Aspect. See [[THE_ASPECTS]].

### The Seed as Filter — and as Sustainer of the Realm
- The Seed's primary function is as a **filter**: it draws **Ambient Chaos** from the realm and converts it into Essence. See [[CHAOS_STATES_AND_ESSENCE]] for what Ambient Chaos actually is.
- The passive output of this process — **Stale Essence** — is produced in quantities too small and diffuse to use directly. It is immediately drawn back into the realm's structure, mixed with ambient Stable Chaos, and reintegrated into the energy permeating the world.
- This is not waste — it is the realm's sustenance loop. The Stale Essence of billions of living beings continuously feeds back into the Ordered Chaos that keeps the realm alive and radiating Stable Chaos outward.
- **Living beings are not merely inhabitants of the realm. They are part of what keeps it alive.** If all Seeds were extinguished, the loop would break, the realm would dim, and eventually collapse.
- The Seed can store Essence — but as a personal reservoir, it is deeply inefficient:
  - It is tiny relative to what practitioners need.
  - Essence stored in the raw Seed leaks out of the being slowly and passively.
- Because of these limits, experienced practitioners do not rely on the raw Seed as a reservoir. They use it as a filter to feed a **Core (Nexus)**.

### Corruption Containment
- Filtering Ambient Chaos through the Seed too rapidly allows raw Chaos to infiltrate the Seed before conversion is complete. Raw Chaos inside the Seed damages it directly — this is **[[CORRUPTION_AND_VERITY|Corruption]]**.
- Corruption is **contained** within the individual Seed by the same Ordered Chaos properties that govern physical separation. The laws of separation are load-bearing at every scale. Corruption cannot bleed outward from one Seed into the Ambient Chaos or into a nearby practitioner's Seed.
- Corruption is a **personal catastrophe**, not a contagion.

## What Happens at Death — Three Tiers

**Tier 1 — Ordinary beings (no Alchemy practice):**
- The Chaos Seed is composed of the same stable chaos as the realm itself.
- At death, it **dissolves back into stable chaos** — returning to the base material of the realm.

**Tier 2 — Those who have taken the First Step into [[MAGIC_OVERVIEW|Alchemy]]:**
- The practitioner has begun using their Seed deliberately.
- At death, the Seed **releases into pure Essence** rather than dissolving.

**Tier 3 — Those who achieved [[MAGIC_OVERVIEW|Fusion]]:**
- The rarest outcome.
- The immense power accumulated in the Core does not dissipate or release as Essence.
- Instead, it **crystallizes rapidly** into a single mundane-looking physical object — a **[[FRAGMENTS_OF_TRUTH|Fragment of Truth]]**.

## The Core (Nexus)
- Practitioners of Alchemy build a **Core** (also called a **Nexus**) within or alongside the Seed.
- Formed by circulating Essence in a continuous loop inside the Seed over time.
- The Core is a stable structure that holds far more Essence than the raw Seed and does not leak.
- See **[[MAGIC_OVERVIEW]]** for full details.
