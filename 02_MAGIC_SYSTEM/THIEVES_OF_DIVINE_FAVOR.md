# Thieves of Divine Favor

## Summary
"Thief of Divine Favor" is the official designation — and the heresy charge — applied to humans who have absorbed a god's Essence without completing the divine transaction and subsequently refuse to acknowledge the god's authority. The Essence strengthens their Cores regardless of whether the transaction is completed. The gods consider this theft. The Inquisition is their instrument of recovery and punishment.

---

## How It Happens

- During the threading process, a god sends a filament of Essence toward a mortal — targeted at those with bright Chaos Seeds.
- The mortal's Seed absorbs the Essence automatically, like a flame absorbs oil. This happens before any transaction is completed.
- If the mortal does not respond — does not decode the dream, does not send Essence back, does not name the god — the filament remains present but unclaimed. The god loses ownership. The Essence is simply absorbed into the mortal's Core.
- The mortal benefits: their Core is stronger than it would otherwise be. They have received divine augmentation without divine obligation.
- Some mortals understand exactly what happened and choose not to complete the transaction — a deliberate refusal of divine authority.
- Others never know a god reached for them at all.

---

## The Metaphysics of Theft

The realm's Ordered Chaos severs divine ownership of Essence at the boundary — not as a hostile act, but as structural reality. The realm's laws do not recognize divine ownership. They apply to everything that crosses the boundary without exception.

This means that Theft is not a violation of any cosmic law. It is a mortal claiming something the universe itself does not protect. The Inquisition's pursuit of Thieves enforces a theological and moral position; it does not enforce a metaphysical one. The universe is agnostic.

For a Thief in the active sense — a mortal who recognized divine Essence and consciously refused the Covenant — this is precisely the point. The refusal is a metaphysical claim: *my Verity is sufficient. No external authority is needed.*

---

## Why Gods Pursue Them

- A god's Essence wandering unclaimed inside the realm is a real loss — a piece of themselves, permanently beyond reach.
- More importantly: a mortal who has received divine Essence but refuses to acknowledge the god represents a direct challenge to the theological authority of the church and the divine order.
- The message of refusing the transaction is: *I took what you offered and I owe you nothing.*
- Under the Laws of Silence, gods cannot act directly against individuals inside the realm. They work through their blessed, who escalate to the [[INQUISITION]].

---

## The Process

1. God detects unclaimed Essence inside the realm (through awareness of their own filaments).
2. God sends a vision to their blessed identifying the Thief — or the general area where they sense the unclaimed Essence.
3. Blessed escalate to the Inquisition.
4. Inquisition locates, investigates, and moves against the Thief.

---

## The Charge

- Official designation: **Thief of Divine Favor**
- Classification: heresy
- The charge is both theological (refusing divine authority) and practical (possessing unclaimed divine Essence, which the church considers stolen property).

---

## Spectrum of Thieves

- **Accidental**: received a dream, absorbed the Essence, never decoded it. May not know they are a Thief. Still hunted if the god detects the loss.
- **Passive refusers**: decoded the dream, understood a god reached for them, chose not to respond. Deliberate but not organized.
- **Active thieves**: like the **[[SECT_OF_ZECHYR]]** — deliberately seek out divine Essence to absorb without completing the transaction. Organized. Sophisticated. Have operated for centuries.
