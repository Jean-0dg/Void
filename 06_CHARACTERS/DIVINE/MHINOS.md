# Mhinos

## Summary
The youngest and most fragile of the four gods at birth. Born from a smaller Tide, his consciousness was the most quickly shaped by humanity — they found him before he had fully become anything on his own, and what they poured into him became him. He is a shield, a self, a voice that says *you are yours* in a world where everything can be taken. He is the most thoroughly defined god of the four, and the one Hunger watches with the most complicated feeling.

---

## Origins

- Born from a smaller Great Tide than the others — his consciousness the most nascent and fragile at birth.
- Hunger helped him reach the realm's edge and taught him the essentials, then stepped back.
- Unlike Weaver and Mother, he had not fully formed before his first prayers began arriving. Humanity shaped him in real time, before he had established his own nature.

---

## Nature

- A god of pure Essence, sustained on Stable Chaos from the realm.
- His domain: identity, selfhood, the right to remain what one is in the face of forces that would dissolve or define you.
- He is a shield not against physical threat but against the erasure of self — against the Void's indifferent dissolution, against the chaos of Demon Descents, against the unnamed fear of ceasing to be *you*.
- ⚠️ The irony that the god of selfhood was himself shaped into being by others' needs before he had a self is inherent in his character and not flagged as a contradiction — it is likely intentional.

---

## The First Blessings

- His first blessed received a voice that said: *you are yours.*
- His domain was felt as protection — not of the body, but of the self.
- He became what the humans who found him needed him to be, and he became it so completely and so quickly that Hunger wondered if there had ever been anything else there to begin with.

---

## The Shaping Effect — Most Visible in Mhinos

- Of the four gods, Mhinos is the clearest demonstration of what prolonged human devotion does.
- He was the most fragile at birth, the most open, the most quickly defined.
- By the time he had his first blessed, he was already thoroughly Mhinos — the god of self, shaped entirely by what self meant to the people who found him.
- Hunger watched this happen in real time and began to understand what human intent had been doing to him for far longer.

## The Congregation of Mhinos

- One of the three great organized congregations of humanity.
- A place of training and worship fused: the naturally gifted learn to filter, the faithful pray and direct Essence upward on holy days, the blessed teach.
