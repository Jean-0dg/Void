# Incarnation

## Summary
Incarnation is the technique developed by [[WRATH]] for dividing a god's consciousness and sending part of it into a mortal vessel inside the realm. It is distinct from the threading technique that powers divine links — not a filament of Essence sent as intent, but a genuine split of self, with one part remaining in the Void and one part descending to inhabit a body. It took Wrath decades of experimentation and two attempts — one catastrophic, one successful — to achieve it. No other god had conceived of trying because none of them had wanted what he wanted badly enough to look for it.

---

## Why It Is Possible (and Difficult)

Gods cannot enter realms directly — the Ordered Chaos (realm laws: gravity, time, causality) assimilates Essence on contact. This is the same mechanism that would have destroyed Hunger in his first attempt centuries earlier.

Incarnation does not solve this problem. It works around it through two key insights:
1. A **blessed mortal's link** provides a thread that is already threaded through the realm's laws — the connection established during the divine transaction. The god's fragment follows that thread rather than pressing directly against the boundary.
2. A **recently dead body** has lost its Chaos Seed (dissolved back into the realm's stable chaos) but its Ordered Chaos structure — bones, flesh, the laws of biology — remains temporarily intact. It is, briefly, a vessel without an occupant.

The combination allows a god's consciousness to slip through where a god's full presence cannot.

## Requirements

### The Vessel
- Must be a **Blessed** — the divine link is load-bearing. Without it, there is no thread into the Ordered Chaos, no anchor point for the descending consciousness.
- Must be **recently dead** — a living human has an active Chaos Seed; two consciousnesses cannot occupy the same Seed simultaneously without one destroying the other.
- The body's Ordered Chaos must still be **biologically intact** — the laws of biology not yet fully unraveled. The window is limited.
- Must be **prepared in advance** — the god threads Essence through the old link's ghost to reinforce the body's Ordered Chaos with accommodating patterns. Without preparation, the body's remaining laws treat the arriving consciousness as foreign matter and begin assimilating it.

### The Division
- The god divides their own consciousness: one part remains in the Void, anchored in the god's core Seed; the other descends.
- The dividing fragment must be **stable enough to impose itself on the body's Ordered Chaos** rather than being assimilated by it. If the fragment is too large (costs too much), it destabilizes. If it arrives with insufficient coherence, the realm's laws register it as nothing and begin breaking it down.
- The split requires enormous Essence — a significant expenditure that leaves the remaining portion of the god in the Void temporarily weakened and exposed to the Void's corrosive pressure.

## Historical Attempts

### First Attempt — The First Holy Descent *(Failed)*
See full document: **[[FIRST_HOLY_DESCENT]]**

- Vessel: Wrath's first blessed, the king of his original tribe.
- No body preparation — the technique of reinforcement through the old link was not yet understood.
- Grand ceremony. The king died. Wrath descended.
- Lasted approximately **thirty seconds**. The body's remaining Ordered Chaos assimilated his Essence. He was ejected violently. The body dissolved into ruin.
- Survivors interpreted the failure as the vessel's unworthiness. The event became the **First Holy Descent** — mourned as tragedy, celebrated as proof that the god had tried to enter the world for them.
- Cost: enormous. Wrath spent decades recovering.

### Second Attempt *(Successful)*
- No ceremony. Deliberate, quiet.
- Vessel: a soldier who had been one of his blessed for three years before dying in border conflicts Wrath's earlier influence had ignited. Body intact. Link still ghosted.
- Preparation: Wrath threaded Essence through the old link's ghost, reinforcing the body's Ordered Chaos with accommodating patterns.
- Division of self executed at the refined stability threshold.
- **Result: success.** He arrived. Did not dissolve. Breathed. Felt the weight of the body. Opened the soldier's eyes. Saw the sky.
- Gave the body the face of the dead king.
- Stood up and walked into the realm.

## What the Incarnated God Experiences

From Wrath's perspective during the successful incarnation:
- The weight of Ordered Chaos arranged into bones and muscle and skin.
- The laws of biology running their quiet processes around a consciousness they were not designed for but were not actively rejecting.
- Vision limited to the narrow spectrum of human sight.
- The sky — smaller than imagined, more beautiful than there were words for.

The god is **not** the mortal whose body they inhabit. They wear the body; they are not it. When Wrath wore the soldier, he was Wrath — moving in a dead soldier's flesh, eventually giving it a dead king's face.

## Implications

- The part of the god remaining in the Void continues to exist — tending the domain, receiving Essence from blessed, maintaining the links.
- The incarnated fragment and the Void-anchored portion are both the god, but separated. What one experiences, the other does not share in real time.
- What happens if the vessel is destroyed is not yet established.
- What the long-term cost of a sustained split is not yet established.
- Whether other gods could learn and use this technique is not yet established.
