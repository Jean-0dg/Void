# The Inquisition

## Summary
The Inquisition is the institution through which the churches enforce divine authority on earth — specifically the detection, investigation, and prosecution of Thieves of Divine Favor and other heretics. Under the Laws of Silence, gods cannot act directly against individuals inside the realm. The Inquisition is how they reach anyway.

---

## Function

- Receives reports from the blessed of the gods when unclaimed Essence is detected.
- Investigates suspected Thieves of Divine Favor.
- Hunts organized heretical groups — most notably the [[SECT_OF_ZECHYR]], which has been on their radar for centuries.
- Broader mandate likely extends to other theological crimes, though the scope is not yet fully established.

---

## Structure and Relationship to the Churches

- Operates alongside (and presumably under the authority of) the churches of the Silent Era gods.
- Whether it answers to one church, all of them collectively, or has its own independent authority structure is not yet established.
- The Five Orders and the Inquisition's relationship is not yet established.
