# Fragments of Truth

## Summary
Fragments of Truth are unique, powerful artifacts created when a **Patri-Alchemist (Stage 13)** dies. Upon death, the fused Nexus does not dissolve — it crystallizes entirely, preserving the accumulated Essence and the imprint of that practitioner's Novel Form in a single mundane-looking object. Each Fragment is one-of-a-kind. They are the most sacred and dangerous relics of the Great Houses.

## Details

### Origin
- Created only upon the death of a **Patri-Alchemist (Stage 13)** or higher — practitioners who survived Fusion and forged a Novel Form.
- The fused Nexus does not dissolve or release as Essence at death — it **crystallizes entirely**, locking in the accumulated Essence and the echo of the Novel Form.
- The crystallized object carries the **imprint of what the practitioner was** — their unique Fusion, their accumulated power, their identity at the moment of death.
- Appearance: mundane. The object itself looks ordinary. The power within is not.

### Properties
- Each Fragment is **unique** — no two are alike in form, power, or cost. Novel Forms are unrepeatable; neither are their Fragments.
- Each grants **incredible power**.
- Each comes with a **terrible cost** — the nature of the cost varies by Fragment.
- Described as "a final echo of the soul that created it."

### Ownership and Inheritance
- Fragments are passed down within **families and organizations** — most prominently the **[[FACTION_OVERVIEW|Great Houses]]**.
- They are treated as sacred relics — the most prized possessions a Great House can hold.
- Their dangerous nature means they are not wielded casually.
