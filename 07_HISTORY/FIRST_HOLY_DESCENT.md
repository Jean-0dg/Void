# The First Holy Descent

## Summary
The First Holy Descent is the name given by Wrath's followers to his first attempt at incarnation — a catastrophic failure that was witnessed by his gathered subjects, interpreted as tragedy, and transformed into the founding sacred event of his religion. The god entered the body of his first blessed (the king), was ejected by the realm's laws within thirty seconds, and left behind a dissolved corpse. The survivors concluded the vessel had been unworthy, not that the technique had failed.

---

## What Actually Happened

- Wrath had developed the Division of Self technique but had not yet understood the need to prepare the vessel in advance.
- He organized a grand ceremony — using his understanding of human psychology to make the scale and solemnity feel confirmatory. The king's subjects gathered. Priests stood in formation.
- The king died as part of the ritual. His Chaos Seed dissolved. His body remained.
- Wrath descended.
- For approximately **thirty seconds**, it worked. He felt the realm's laws. He felt the texture of the dead king's hands. He saw his subjects kneeling, their expressions of awe almost overwhelming.
- Then the realm's Ordered Chaos found him — assimilating his Essence the same way it would have assimilated any god pressing too directly against the realm's boundary.
- He was ejected violently. The body collapsed into a ruin of melted flesh.
- Wrath returned to the Void, vastly depleted, requiring decades of recovery.

## What Witnesses Believed

- The king's subjects saw their king dissolve and the divine presence vanish.
- They interpreted this as: the god had tried to honor them with his physical presence; the king's mortal nature had been insufficient to hold him; the vessel was unworthy, not the god.
- This narrative absolved the god of failure and elevated the king to tragic martyr.
- The event was named **The First Holy Descent** and became the founding sacred story of Wrath's congregation.

## Significance

- The interpretation of failure as divine tragedy is the foundational text of Wrath's religion.
- Wrath did not correct this interpretation — it served him.
- The event established precedent in the minds of his followers that the god *wants* to enter the world and is *prevented* by the inadequacy of mortal vessels — creating a religious drive toward producing worthier vessels.
- This precedent will matter when Wrath successfully incarnates.
