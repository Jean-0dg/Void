# World Overview

High-level summary of the entire world. Updated as canon is established.

## Cosmology
The world exists within (or was born from) **[[THE_VOID]]** — an infinite expanse of primordial Chaos Energy. Realms form and dissolve within the Void on ~300-year cycles. Gods are born from **[[GREAT_TIDES]]**, periodic events where Void energy transforms into pure **Essence** and becomes self-aware.

## History
The world's history is divided into three eras. See **[[ERAS_OVERVIEW]]** for full documentation.

- **The Nascent Era** — Five gods. Three great human congregations. Wrath's rise, the subduing of three gods, the Incarnation. Ended with the Great Fracture.
- **The Great Fracture** — Wrath's avatar exploded. Permanent metaphysical damage. Lost Continents. Deep Void tears.
- **The Silent Era (current)** — Gods operate under Paradox's Laws of Silence. Seven Kingdoms. Five Orders. Great Houses. 18 Forms. Deep-Void creatures emerge every Great Tide.

## Current World State
- **Political:** Seven Kingdoms. Five Orders. Great Houses (holders of Fragments of Truth).
- **Magic:** Alchemy with 18 official Forms across 5 tiers. Core → Form → Fusion.
- **Divine:** Gods exist but are silent by law. Paradox watches from the furthest edge of his domain.
- **Geography:** Lost Continents are uninhabitable. Permanent Void tears complicate every Great Tide.
