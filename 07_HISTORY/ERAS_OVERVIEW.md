# Eras Overview

A high-level timeline of the world's history, organized by era.

---

## The Nascent Era
*Before the Great Fracture. Survives only in the oldest, most corrupted myths.*

**Key events:**
- The first Great Tide and the birth of Hunger (the first god)
- Hunger's discovery of the threading technique and the first divine transaction
- The birth of Weaver, Mother, and Mhinos — the three active gods
- The three great congregations: Weaver, Mother, Mhinos organized humanity around their temples
- The growth of three great tribes and the tribal resource conflicts
- The birth of Wrath — guided by the three active gods, not Hunger
- Wrath's conflict feedback loop, his deception of the three gods, and his extraction of their techniques
- The development and first attempt at Incarnation — the First Holy Descent
- Wrath's successful Incarnation; his physical empire
- The subduing of Weaver, Mother, and Mhinos within Wrath's domain
- The accelerated Great Tide
- The ancient Abomination's consumption of the three gods → becoming a Demon
- Weaver's last vision to her blessed
- The Demon's injection into Wrath's avatar link
- **The Great Fracture** — the avatar explodes; permanent tears; realm metaphysics deformed
- Paradox destroys the Corrupted Wrath
- Paradox gifts humanity the Forms
- Establishment of the Laws of Silence

**What is remembered:**
- Almost nothing accurately. The Nascent Era survives in corrupted myths maintained by the Five Orders more as tradition than understanding.
- The First Holy Descent is recorded as tragedy and miracle. Its true nature is not understood.
- The names Weaver, Mother, and Mhinos may persist in religion — what happened to them is not known.

---

## The Great Fracture
*The boundary event. Marked by the detonation of Wrath's avatar.*

See **[[GREAT_FRACTURE]]** for full documentation.

**Immediate consequences:**
- Permanent deformation of the realm's metaphysics
- Some Tide tears are now permanent
- Future Tide tears sometimes connect to the deep Void
- The Lost Continents: devastated, uninhabitable, still bleeding Chaos
- Humanity nearly extinguished

---

## The Silent Era
*The current era. Named for the Laws of Silence governing divine behavior.*

**Key features:**
- Gods operate under the Laws of Silence — minimal contact with humanity, no avatars, no empires
- Humanity rebuilt around the **Seven Kingdoms** and **Five Orders**
- **Great Houses** defined by their Fragments of Truth
- The **18 Forms** documented, organized, named, and tiered
- Alchemical practice is mature but regulated
- Every Great Tide brings something worse than before through the permanent deep-Void tears
- The Lost Continents remain uninhabitable and contested
- Paradox watches from the furthest edge of his domain

**What humanity knows (approximately):**
- The gods are distant — their silence interpreted as mystery, not law
- The Forms and Alchemy are understood as human achievement, not divine gift
- The Nascent Era is corrupted myth
- The Great Fracture is history — its cause not understood
