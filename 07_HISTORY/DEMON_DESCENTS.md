# Demon Descents

## Summary
Demon Descents are catastrophic events caused by Great Tides that tear rifts through existing realms rather than producing gods, realms, or abominations. These rifts allow inhabitants of other realms to cross into the affected world. Humans named these events "Demon Descents" because most of the otherworldly beings they encountered were classified as demons.

## Details

- Caused by a Great Tide variant that tears through existing realms instead of converging into a new entity or structure.
- The tears are rifts — openings between realms.
- The beings that cross through are not necessarily malevolent by nature; they were simply named "demons" by humans who encountered them.
- The classification of "demon" is a **human cultural label**, not a precise cosmological category.
