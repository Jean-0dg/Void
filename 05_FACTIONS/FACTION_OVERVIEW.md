# Faction Overview

## Summary
Overview of major organizations and power structures in the world. To be expanded as factions are defined.

---

## Great Houses

**Status:** canonical (existence confirmed; one named)

- The Great Houses are the families and organizations that hold **[[FRAGMENTS_OF_TRUTH|Fragments of Truth]]**.
- They are defined in part by their ownership of these relics — the crystallized power of deceased alchemical Masters.
- Fragments are their most sacred and dangerous possessions.

### House Aurelian
- **Status:** canonical
- A Matriarchy. Controls the highest tier of **Soul Healers** — practitioners trained in the Light + Fire combination required to cauterize Corruption wounds.
- Soul Healing is their institutional power and their primary source of political leverage.
- "The Aurelian Lance" is referenced as a named Structured Art — suggesting the House also has a martial tradition.

## The Three Great Tribes

**Status:** canonical (existence and conflict confirmed; names and details provisional)

- The three great congregations organized themselves around the temples of Weaver, Mother, and Mhinos — each with a corresponding human population.
- As populations grew and resources strained, the tribes pressed against each other's edges.
- Religion became inseparable from tribal identity, which became inseparable from resource conflict.
- Weaver's warnings were reinterpreted as confirmation of enemy threat. Mother's harvests were read as divine judgment on neighbors. Mhinos's theology of individual sovereignty became justification for taking what was needed and calling it self-preservation.
- The three gods could not calm this kind of conflict — it was not an external threat like the Great Tides. It was internal human failure operating in the space between divine interventions.

### Open Questions
- ❓ What are the names of the three tribes/civilizations?
- ❓ What is the current state of the conflict in either story's timeline — ongoing war, uneasy peace, aftermath?

## Minor Tribes

**Status:** canonical (existence confirmed; details provisional)

- Dozens of smaller tribes existed in the shadows of the three great congregations.
- They were slowly being pushed from the best land by the expanding weight of the larger groups.
- Wrath's first blessed came from one of these minor tribes — a king whose fury at being displaced was the exact emotional match for what Wrath sent through the dream.

---

## Congregation of Wrath

**Status:** canonical (existence confirmed; details provisional)

- Grew from Wrath's first blessed and their descendants.
- Founded on the **First Holy Descent** as its sacred origin event — the god attempted to enter the world, the vessel was unworthy, the king became a martyr.
- This foundational narrative implies a religious drive toward producing worthier vessels — humans capable of sustaining divine presence.
- The congregation does not yet know that Wrath has successfully incarnated through different means.

## The Five Orders

**Status:** canonical (existence confirmed; details provisional)

- Major organizations of the Silent Era, alongside the Great Houses.
- Formed in the aftermath of the Great Fracture as humanity rebuilt.
- Maintain the oldest surviving myths of the Nascent Era — more as tradition than as understanding.
- Their relationship to Alchemy, the Great Houses, and the Seven Kingdoms is not yet established.

## The Achelan Empire

**Status:** canonical (existence confirmed; details provisional)

- The primary political power of the Silent Era, or at least a dominant one.
- The 15-stage Alchemy classification system originates from within the Achelan Empire — it is described as lying "at the heart of the Achelan Empire's power structure."
- Nexus formation and the Alchemy path are not just cultural — they are institutional, integrated into the Empire's power hierarchy.
- Relationship to the Seven Kingdoms, Five Orders, and Great Houses not yet established. The Empire may encompass or predate the Seven Kingdoms, or be one of them.

## The Seven Kingdoms

**Status:** canonical (existence confirmed; names and details provisional)

- The political structure of the Silent Era.
- Formed after the collapse of Wrath's empire and the chaos of the Great Fracture.
- Their relationship to the Five Orders, the Great Houses, and the three old congregations is not yet established.

## The Academies

**Status:** canonical (existence confirmed; details provisional)

- Institutions where Alchemy students train and undergo Resonance Trials to find their Aspect.
- "Guessing" — choosing an Aspect — is described as the most stressful part of an Academy student's life.
- Their relationship to the churches, the Five Orders, and the Great Houses is not yet established.

---

## The Legions

**Status:** canonical (existence confirmed; details provisional)

- The military structure of the realm.
- Stage 3 (The Hardened) is the minimum rank required for military service.
- Type A Aspects (Tiers 3–5) are described as the backbone of Legion forces — reliable, fast to train.
- Their relationship to the Seven Kingdoms is not yet established.
