# Corruption and Verity

## Summary
Corruption is what happens when the Chaos Seed is exposed to unfiltered Void energy — either from filtering too fast, or from bypassing the filter entirely when reserves run empty. It is not one thing. There are three distinct outcomes depending on the nature, speed, and degree of exposure. Verity — the human sense of self — is the natural defense. The stronger a practitioner's Verity, the more resistant they are to all three forms.

---

## How Corruption Occurs

### Passive (Filtering Too Fast)
- When a practitioner draws Purified Energy faster than the filter can process it, raw Chaos infiltrates the Seed before conversion is complete.
- Cumulative. Not instantaneous. Gets worse over time.
- The primary danger of ambitious or undisciplined practice.

### Active (Bypassing the Filter)
- When reserves are empty, a practitioner can bypass the filter entirely and draw raw Chaos directly.
- Produces immediate power.
- Causes acute Corruption — the effects arrive fast and are more severe than passive accumulation.

---

## The Three Outcomes

### 1. The Beast *(Acute Corruption)*
- The mind is wiped entirely.
- The practitioner becomes a mindless creature driven purely by the hunger for energy.
- No recovery known.
- Typically the result of sudden, massive Void exposure — bypassing the filter at high volume, or being directly exposed to raw Chaos during a Tide.

### 2. The Scarred *(Somatic Corruption)*
- The mind survives intact. The body does not.
- Physical mutation: tentacles, burns, structural deformations that reflect the Chaos assimilating biological matter.
- Requires surgical intervention to halt progression.
- The practitioner remains themselves — but visibly marked.

### 3. The Herald — The Dissonant *(Insidious Corruption)*
- The most dangerous outcome. The Seed is "planted" — a fragment of Void energy embeds itself without destroying the mind.
- The practitioner retains full consciousness and cognitive function.
- Their conscience is inverted: what they once valued, they now work against. What they once protected, they now threaten.
- They become a **Dissonant** — a living gateway for the Void. Their presence destabilizes the Ordered Chaos around them over time.
- This is insidious because it is undetectable without specialized methods. A Dissonant looks, sounds, and reasons like themselves. The inversion is internal.

---

## Corruption and the Cultivation Path

Corruption is not a stage of progression. It is a consequence of exceeding one's capacity — and it runs alongside the cultivation path from the very first day. Every practitioner, at every stage, carries this risk. The nature of what it does to them changes as they grow.

### The Fixed Filtration Limit
- Every alchemist has a fixed maximum of raw Chaos their Nexus can safely process at once.
- **This limit does not increase with Realm advancement.** Higher Realm practitioners grow more efficient — they rarely approach their limit under normal conditions. But the limit is always there.
- In extremity — desperate combat, deliberate overextension, choosing to draw more than the filter can handle — the ceiling reasserts for everyone at every stage.

### Realm-Based Corruption Progression

**Lower Realms (Realm 1–2) — The Body Bears It**
- At early stages, the body absorbs the impact of overexposure first.
- Corruption at these Realms tends toward the physical — mutations, burns, structural deformations. The Scarred outcome is the characteristic risk.
- The mind survives. The body pays.
- Early-stage corrupted entities are dangerous but containable. The Corruption threat classification begins at **Dangerous** for good reason.

**Middle Realms (Realm 3–4) — The Threshold of Inversion**
- As the practitioner's Verity deepens and their Nexus architecture grows more complex, the body becomes more resistant. The Corruption cannot anchor in flesh as easily.
- Instead it seeks weaker structure: the edges of identity.
- The Dissonant outcome becomes progressively more likely from Realm 3 onward. The Scarred outcome does not disappear — it becomes rarer relative to the insidious alternative.

**Higher Realms (Realm 5) — The Most Dangerous Transformation**
- At Fusion Realm, Corruption that survives the body's resistance and the practitioner's Verity reaches the Novel Form itself.
- A Corrupted Patri-Alchemist or Deus Alchemist is not a Beast in the traditional sense. Their Novel Form remains. Their power remains. Their goals invert.
- The higher the Realm, the more devastating the corrupted entity if transformation occurs. A Corrupted Deus Alchemist is not a classification problem — it is an extinction event for everything within range.
- The 7-level threat classification was built partly to account for this escalation.

## Verity — The Defense

- **Verity** is the human-named concept of the sense of self — the affirmation of one's own reality.
- A natural, internal quality. Cannot be externally granted, though practice may strengthen it.
- Functions as resistance to all three Corruption types:
  - Strong Verity = slower accumulation, greater tolerance, higher chance of surviving acute exposure without full Beast-state.
  - The Dissonant outcome is most likely in practitioners whose sense of self was already fragmented or uncertain before Void exposure.
- The Void, by its nature, erodes structure and identity. Verity is the answer the human soul makes to that erosion.

## Soul Healing

- A rare medical discipline: the "cauterizing" of spiritual wounds caused by Corruption.
- Requires a combination of **Light + Fire** Aspects applied with exceptional precision.
- Used to halt or reverse The Scarred outcome; potentially applicable to early-stage Dissonant cases.
- **[[HOUSE_AURELIAN|House Aurelian]]** controls the highest tier of Soul Healers — a Matriarchy that holds this knowledge as institutional power.
- Grand Arrays can be configured as **Soul Healing Zones** — large-scale applications for treating multiple practitioners or severe cases.

## Containment — Why Corruption Doesn't Spread

The Chaos Seed is composed of Ordered Chaos. The same property of Ordered Chaos that enforces physical separation — the laws that prevent two bodies from occupying the same space — prevents Corruption from bleeding outward from one Seed to another or into the Ambient Chaos of the surrounding environment.

Corruption is a **personal catastrophe, not a contagion.** The laws of separation are load-bearing at every scale: physical, metaphysical, biological.

Additionally: Essence used in alchemical techniques is **consumed**, not released. What returns to the world after a technique fires is the energy of the effect itself — the fire, the force, the impact. Not the channeled Essence that produced it. Corrupted Essence is consumed in the act of casting and never re-enters the ambient loop. The ambient environment cannot be corrupted through use.
