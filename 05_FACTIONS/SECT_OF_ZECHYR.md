# The Sect of Zechyr

## Summary
The Sect of Zechyr is a secret, centuries-old organization operating entirely outside the divine order. Named for Zechyr — the follower of Weaver who received her last vision at the Great Fracture — the Sect holds a heretical cosmological doctrine that directly contradicts every major church: humans came first, gods came after, and the established faiths are structures the gods built to secure Essence from a humanity they depend on. They shelter branded heretics, appear rarely and without trace, and maintain texts that are more accurate than anything the churches teach.

---

## Origin

- Founded by or in the legacy of **[[ZECHYR]]**, a fervent follower of Weaver who received her last vision at the moment of the Great Fracture.
- That vision: *something old at the edge of the dark. Watching. Still there.* — Weaver pointing, in the only way she had left, toward Paradox.
- The Sect's cosmological understanding may be rooted in what Zechyr received — a glimpse, however partial, of the true structure of divine existence from the perspective of a god who had seen it from the outside.

---

## Core Doctrine

### 1. The Void as True Origin
- The Void is the source of all existence — infinite, ever-changing, from which all order eventually emerges.
- It has birthed countless realms throughout eternity. Most are unstable and short-lived.

### 2. Humans as Children of the Void
- The human realm was an anomaly: a particularly stable and ordered convergence of Chaos Energy that produced tangible, lasting structure.
- Humans — born within this realm, carrying Chaos Seeds — are the true "children of the Void." They have what no god has: physical form. Existence inside the Ordered Chaos.

### 3. Gods as Dreams of the Void
- Gods are beings of pure Essence that manifest during Great Tides.
- Unable to survive long in raw Void chaos, they are drawn to the human realm for the protection its emanating order provides.
- Each god constructs what the Sect calls a **"cage"** — their term for a divine domain — a structure that preserves their consciousness from dissolution.
- The gods need humanity's ordered reality to survive. Without the realm — without the symbiosis loop of living beings sustaining the realm — the gods would have nothing.

### 4. Humans Preceded Gods — The Central Heresy
- **Humans came first.** The realm formed and was populated before any god was born from it.
- The gods did not create humanity. They arrived afterward, drawn by necessity.
- The established religions present gods as creators and benefactors. The Sect teaches they are dependents.

### 5. The Manipulation Doctrine
- The mainstream faiths are not divine truth. They are structures the gods built to secure worship and Essence from humanity.
- The gods pose as creators. In truth, it is humanity that sustains the conditions of their existence.

## Operations

### What They Are Not
- Not a blatant or visible organization. They do not appear openly or make themselves known.
- Their operations are surgical and infrequent — they surface rarely, only when something specific draws them out.

### What They Actually Do
- They identify mortals branded as **[[THIEVES_OF_DIVINE_FAVOR|Thieves of Divine Favor]]** by the Inquisition.
- They appear quietly, leaving no trace, and extract or shelter those heretics before the Inquisition closes in.
- This is how they grow: they take people the gods have already marked, people who carry divine Essence in their Cores without divine obligation, and bring them in.
- They use **unique techniques** — not yet established in detail — sophisticated enough that centuries of Inquisition pursuit have never identified a single member.

### Texts
- Doctrine is written in **coded language** on specially prepared parchment.
- Texts include **cosmological maps** of the Void's structure and its relationship to the tangible world.
- Senior members claim to have developed **techniques to manipulate the boundary between order and chaos**. Whether these have actual power is unproven.

---

## Relationship to the Inquisition

- On the Inquisition's radar for centuries. Never dismantled. Never identified.
- The Inquisition knows they exist. They do not know who, where, or how.

## What They May Know

- The founding vision points toward Paradox — the oldest god, outside the current divine order entirely.
- ⚠️ The Sect may be the only human institution in the Silent Era with any thread — however corrupted by time — pointing toward Paradox.
- Whether current members understand this, or carry it as unexplained tradition, is not yet established.
