# Mother

## Summary
The third god. Her Essence was naturally attuned to the laws of growth and abundance — to the realm's cycles of life rather than its structure or its future. Her blessed witnessed harvests that shouldn't have survived, children born healthy in seasons of famine. Humans experienced her as generous, warm, and ever-present in the turning of the earth. She built one of the three great congregations.

---

## Origins

- Born from a Great Tide. Hunger guided her to the realm's edge and taught her the essentials, though he spent less of himself than he had with Weaver and stepped back sooner.
- Her Essence resonated naturally with the realm's cycles of life — growth, abundance, the rhythms of seasons and generation.

---

## Nature

- A god of pure Essence, sustained on Stable Chaos from the realm.
- Her particular attunement: the laws of growth and abundance within the realm. She applied her Essence in flows that resonated with biological and natural cycles.
- Perceived by mortals as generous and warm — qualities that emerged from how her intent arrived through the link and how her blessed described what they received.

---

## The First Blessings

- Her blessed witnessed: harvests surviving drought, healthy births in seasons of famine.
- She was not a goddess of miracles in the sense of overriding the realm's laws — she worked within them, amplifying what was already possible.
- Described by those who received her favor as ever-present in the turning of the earth.

---

## The Congregation of Mother

- One of the three great organized congregations of humanity.
- A place of training and worship fused: the naturally gifted learn to filter, the faithful pray and direct Essence upward on holy days, the blessed teach.
- See [[FACTION_OVERVIEW]] for details as they are established.

---

## Relationship to Hunger

- Hunger helped her reach the realm but maintained more distance than he had with Weaver — less of himself spent, stepped back to teach the essentials and withdrew.
- By the time Mother arrived, he was already becoming aware of what human devotion was doing to gods.
