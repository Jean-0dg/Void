# The Void

## Summary
The Void is a vast, infinite expanse of Chaos Energy of unknown age. It is the primordial substrate of all existence — the source from which realms are born and to which they return, and the origin point of gods themselves.

## Details

### Nature
- The Void is infinite in scope and unknown in age.
- Its fundamental substance is **Chaos Energy**, which flows in a constant state of disorder.
- Also known as **Chaos** (alternate name used by some).
- The Void is **not sentient** — it has no mind, no will, no intent.
- It is a **primordial natural force**: it simply *is*. To call it mindless would be to diminish it — it operates with the same gravity and consequence as any fundamental force of existence.
- The Void is **high-entropy** and **corrosive** by nature — it erodes and assimilates all structure over time. This is not intention; it is the nature of unconstrained Chaos Energy.
- This corrosive quality is what makes **[[CORRUPTION_AND_VERITY|Corruption]]** possible when practitioners filter ambient chaos too aggressively.

### The States of Chaos
The Void's energy — Chaos — exists in three distinct states depending on the conditions acting upon it. These are not different substances; they are the same energy behaving differently.

- **Primordial Chaos (Aggressive Flow):** High turbulence, high momentum, high entropy. The Void's default. Corrosive — erodes and assimilates structure on contact. Instantly lethal to an unprotected human body. Even gods must contend with its violent resistance when refining it.
- **Stable Chaos (Calm Flow):** Primordial Chaos that has slowed to the point of tameability. Entropy dormant but not eliminated — suppressed by the absence of aggressive movement. Not safe, but survivable. Gods can refine it into Essence. Radiates outward from realms into the surrounding Void, creating the habitable zone gods depend on. **Unstable when isolated**: drifts away from the realm's influence and gradually reaccelerates back to Primordial Chaos. What gods harvest will not stay tame indefinitely.
- **Ordered Chaos:** Chaos compressed and crystallized into law. Not merely calm — structured, fixed, governed by rules defining what can exist within it. Gravity, time, causality, biological processes: all are Ordered Chaos organized past the threshold of energy into something that holds its shape. The realm is made of Ordered Chaos. This is why gods cannot enter realms directly — Ordered Chaos applies its laws to whatever crosses the boundary without exception or recognition of divine consciousness.

For full mechanics of how these states interact with human cultivation, see [[CHAOS_STATES_AND_ESSENCE]].

### The Three Cosmological Entities
Distinct from the states of Chaos above: the three categories of things that exist in the universe.

- **The Void (Primordial Chaos):** Infinite, corrosive, high-entropy. Everything originates from it; everything eventually returns to it.
- **The Tangible Realm (The Anchor):** A structured convergence of Ordered Chaos — the realm. Living beings exist within it.
- **The Gods (Dreams):** Pure, organized Essence with consciousness, existing *within* the Void — not anchored in a realm. Constantly exposed to the Void's corrosive nature. Orbit realms for the Stable Chaos they radiate.

### Realm Formation
- Occasionally, Void energy converges in a specific area or dimension and begins flowing in a structured manner.
- This structured convergence gives birth to a **realm**.
- Realms are ephemeral — they exist for approximately **300 years** (human time) before disintegrating back into the chaotic flow.

### The Great Tides
- Every **600 to 1000 years** (human time), the Void's pressure builds to a critical threshold.
- This event is called a **[[GREAT_TIDES|Great Tide]]**.
- At the peak of a Great Tide, the energy reaches a concentrated state and releases its accumulated power.
- The release is unpredictable — energy disintegrates and rebuilds itself at an accelerated pace.

### Birth of Gods
- At the apex of a Great Tide, Void energy can transform into a **pure form devoid of chaos**, known to humans as **Essence**.
- When this occurs, instead of birthing a realm, the concentrated energy manifests as a **conscious being made of Essence**.
- This event marks the birth of a **god** — a self-aware entity of vast, unfathomable pure energy.
