# Canon Log

Running log of confirmed canonical decisions, in order of establishment.

## 2026-05-10

### ✅ The Void — Core Cosmology
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-10
**Source:** Author description

- The Void is an infinite expanse of Chaos Energy of unknown age.
- Also called "Chaos."
- Realms form from structured convergences of Void energy and last ~300 years before dissolving.
- Great Tides occur every 600–1000 years and build to a critical release of accumulated energy.
- At the peak of a Great Tide, Void energy can transform into pure Essence and manifest as a conscious god.
- Gods are beings of pure Essence, self-aware, born from Great Tides.
- The Void is not sentient — no mind, no will, no intent. It is a primordial natural force. Calling it "mindless" is considered a grave understatement; it simply *is*.
- Nothing exists outside the Void. It contains everything.

### ✅ Great Tides — Full Mechanics
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-10

- Great Tides are localized events within the Void, not universe-wide explosions.
- Outcomes are determined by: (1) quantity of chaos accumulated, (2) type of reaction.
- **Realm formation**: massive implosion → chaos solidifies/calcifies → realm forms → Essence-mixed core → living beings with Chaos Seeds.
- **God formation**: ~1/10th the energy of realm formation → explosive purification → vast concentrated Essence becomes conscious → god born.
- **Abomination formation**: failed purification → partial consciousness → entity made of Chaos (lesser god analogue).
- **Demon Descents**: Great Tide tears rifts through existing realms → otherworldly beings invade → humans label them demons.
- **Essence Permeation**: Great Tide diffuses stable Chaos into existing realms → easier Essence production → exceptional births follow.
- Multiple gods can theoretically be born from one Great Tide. Never recorded in history.
- Realms typically last ~300 years. Some fail earlier, some persist. No hard cap.

### ✅ Chaos Seeds (Souls)
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-10

- All living beings carry a Chaos Seed — a fragment of mixed Chaos Energy and Essence.
- Originates from the Essence-mixed core at the heart of a realm-forming Great Tide.
- Human term for it: **soul**.

### ✅ Chaos Seed at Death — Three Tiers
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-10

- **Tier 1 (ordinary beings):** Seed dissolves back into stable chaos of the realm.
- **Tier 2 (First Step Alchemy practitioners):** Seed releases as pure Essence at death.
- **Tier 3 (Fusion Masters):** Seed crystallizes rapidly into a Fragment of Truth.

### ✅ Alchemy (Magic System — Initial Canon)
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-10

- Human term for the practice of refining one's Chaos Seed (also called the "Core" in this context).
- Entry point: "First Step." Highest state: "Fusion."
- Those who achieve Fusion are called Masters.
- Full progression between First Step and Fusion not yet established.

### ✅ Fragments of Truth
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-10

- Artifacts crystallized from the Core of a Master (Fusion-achieved practitioner) at death.
- Shaped by the Master's final thoughts and lifelong obsessions.
- Appear mundane; contain immense power at a terrible cost.
- Unique — no two are alike.
- Described as "a final echo of the soul that created it."
- Held by Great Houses (families and organizations). Most sacred and dangerous relics they possess.

### ✅ Alchemy — Full System Mechanics
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-10

- The Chaos Seed is a **filter**, not the target of refinement. It draws ambient stable chaos and converts it to Essence.
- Raw Seed as reservoir: inefficient. Essence leaks out passively. Tiny capacity.
- **Core (Nexus):** Formed by circulating Essence in a loop inside the Seed. Efficient reservoir. No leakage. Generic until a Form is taken.
- **Forms:** 18 official Forms, 5 tiers. Core takes a Form via intricate Essence flow sustained over time. Permanently specializes the practitioner.
- **Law of Texture / Affinity:** Every Seed has a natural grain. Must be discovered through Resonance Trials (years of meditation). Match → Stage 6+ potential. Mismatch → stalls at Stage 3.
- Choosing a Form is called "Guessing." The most consequential decision in a practitioner's life.
- **Fusion** = highest achievable state. Practitioners who achieve it are Masters. Core crystallizes into Fragment of Truth at death.

### ✅ Corruption and Verity
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-10

- **Corruption:** Raw chaos infiltrates the Seed when filtering is too fast. Cumulative soul damage. Not instantaneous.
- **Verity:** Human-named concept — sense of self, affirmation of one's reality. Natural defense against Corruption. Strong Verity = can climb the path. Weak Verity = at risk of succumbing.

### ✅ Three States of Energy (Cosmological Framework)
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-10

- **The Void (Primordial Chaos):** Corrosive, high-entropy. Erodes and assimilates all structure. Not sentient — this is its nature, not its will. "Seeks" language is metaphorical only.
- **The Tangible Realm (The Anchor):** Pressurized bubble of frozen Void energy. Matter, planets, living beings. Structural shield.
- **The Gods (Dreams):** Pure Essence with consciousness. Exist within the Void. Actively fight Corruption from constant Void exposure. Often drawn to Realms for stability.

### ✅ Gods — Existence in the Void (from Hunger's Origin)
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-13

- Gods experience the Void as constant agony from the moment of birth. The Void erodes them continuously.
- Gods sustain themselves by refining Stable Chaos (radiating from realms) into Essence.
- Gods cannot enter realms. Ordered Chaos (realm laws) would assimilate and unmake their Essence.
- Gods perceive realm-dwelling living beings as tiny points of light.
- Gods have no name at birth. Names require witnesses.
- Gods orbit realms from the exterior; their survival is tied to the realm's health.
- A weakened realm = thinned Stable Chaos = increased agony for nearby gods + Abomination convergence.

### ✅ Realms — Exterior Appearance and Properties (from Hunger's Origin)
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-13

- Realms appear from outside as vast lakes of light suspended in Void darkness — not as bubbles.
- Emit Stable Chaos outward into the surrounding Void.
- Their internal laws (gravity, time, causality) protect mortals but would destroy a god attempting to enter.
- Realm tears from Great Tides are temporary — the realm moves to mend itself.

### ✅ Abominations — Behavior Confirmed
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-13

- Wander the Void on instinct. Not consciously hostile — drawn to Essence like currents toward a drain.
- Individually weaker than gods. But endless.

### ✅ The Symbiosis Loop — Living Beings Sustain Realms
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-13

- Living beings filter ambient chaos through their Seeds and breathe out Essence.
- That Essence is reabsorbed into the realm — a continuous loop sustaining the realm's structure and Stable Chaos output.
- Living beings are not merely inhabitants. They are part of what keeps the realm alive.
- If all Seeds are extinguished: loop breaks → realm dims → realm collapses.

### ✅ Divine Connection Mechanics
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-13

- Gods thread thin Essence filaments *between* realm laws, not against them.
- Filaments target brightest Seeds. Intent arrives as a dream. Most mortals never decode it.
- The Transaction: god sends presence → mortal responds with directed Essence + acknowledgment → god sends full weight of itself → mortal receives and names the god → name spoken = link completes.
- Completed link: god feels mortal's Seed, senses its texture, perceives realm dimly through mortal's senses.
- Too much presence overwhelms and kills the mortal. The link must be held gently.
- Stolen Essence: mortals who absorb the filament without completing the transaction. Essence becomes unclaimed inside the realm. God loses that piece permanently.
- The Blessed: mortals who completed the transaction. The first Blessed taught what they understood — distorted over generations into the first religion.

### ✅ Incarnation — Divine Technique
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-16

- **Division of Self**: one part of the god's consciousness remains in the Void (anchored to core Seed); one part descends into a mortal vessel.
- Vessel requirements: must be a Blessed (link is load-bearing); must be recently dead (living Chaos Seed prevents dual occupancy); body's Ordered Chaos still intact; body must be prepared through old link to accommodate the arriving consciousness.
- Without preparation: realm's Ordered Chaos assimilates the arriving fragment (as in the First Holy Descent).
- The split costs enormous Essence. Leaves the Void-anchored portion weakened temporarily.
- The other gods do not know this is possible.

### ✅ The Three States of Chaos
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- **Primordial Chaos (Aggressive Flow):** High turbulence, high entropy, corrosive. The Void's default. Instantly lethal to humans. Resists god refinement.
- **Stable Chaos (Calm Flow):** Primordial Chaos slowed until entropy is dormant. Survivable. Gods can refine it. Radiates from realms into surrounding Void. Unstable when isolated — gradually reaccelerates back to Primordial when separated from the realm's influence.
- **Ordered Chaos:** Chaos compressed into law. Not energy but structure: gravity, time, causality, biology. The realm is made of it. Gods cannot enter because Ordered Chaos applies its laws to everything at the boundary without exception.
- These are states of the same substance, not different substances.

### ✅ Ambient Chaos — What Alchemists Actually Draw From
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- Alchemists do not draw Primordial Chaos directly. The realm's boundary prevents it; it would destroy an unprotected body.
- **Ambient Chaos** is the blend alchemists draw from: (1) Ordered Chaos (realm structure, present everywhere), (2) Stable Chaos (calm dormant energy within and radiating from the realm), (3) Stale Essence (recycled back into the ambient mix by the sustenance loop).
- Far less corrosive than Primordial Chaos. Compatible with human biology. Effectively inexhaustible in local quantities.

### ✅ Stale Essence — New Term
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- **Stale Essence**: the passive Essence produced continuously by all living beings simply by virtue of being alive and conscious.
- Too small and diffuse to use directly. Immediately reabsorbed into the realm's structure, mixed with Stable Chaos, and integrated into Ambient Chaos.
- The quiet, constant fuel of the realm's sustenance loop.

### ✅ Essence — Fundamental Nature
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- Essence is Chaos with its entropy removed. Not a different substance — Chaos temporarily won from entropy.
- Impermanence is intrinsic: Essence left unattended will degrade. Released Essence will revert to Ambient Chaos. No permanent victory over entropy.
- Gods and alchemists are engaged in the same essential struggle at different scales.
- Essence used in alchemical techniques is **consumed**. What returns to the world is the energy of the technique's effect, not the Essence that fueled it.

### ✅ Corruption Containment
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- Corruption is a personal catastrophe, not a contagion.
- The Chaos Seed is Ordered Chaos. The same laws of separation that prevent physical interpenetration prevent Corruption from bleeding outward to nearby Seeds or into the Ambient Chaos.
- Corrupted Essence used in techniques is consumed, not released — it does not re-enter the ambient loop.
- No pandemic mechanic. No environmental contamination through use.

### ✅ Covenant — New Term
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- **Covenant**: the canonical term for a completed divine transaction — when a mortal recognizes divine Essence, acknowledges its source, and accepts the mutual relationship.
- Previously documented as "completed transaction." Covenant is the precise term.
- Three outcomes for divine Essence inside the realm: Unaware Absorption (gradual reversion), Covenant (link completes, mortal becomes Blessed), Theft (mortal claims with their own Verity, refuses the relationship).

### ✅ The Universe's Agnosticism About Divine Ownership
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- The realm's Ordered Chaos severs divine ownership of Essence at the boundary — not hostile, but structural.
- The realm's laws do not recognize divine ownership. Essence inside the realm belongs to the Verity capable of claiming it.
- Theft of divine Essence violates no cosmic law. The Inquisition enforces a theological position; the universe enforces nothing.

### ✅ Corruption — Realm-Based Scaling
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- Corruption risk runs alongside the cultivation path from Stage 1. It is not a progression stage — it is a consequence of exceeding filtration capacity.
- The Fixed Filtration Limit does not increase with Realm advancement. What changes is efficiency, not ceiling.
- Lower Realms (1–2): Corruption manifests primarily as physical mutation (The Scarred). The body absorbs impact first.
- Middle Realms (3–4): The body resists physical anchoring. The Dissonant outcome becomes progressively more likely as Corruption seeks weaker structure — the edges of identity.
- Higher Realms (5): Corruption that survives body and Verity reaches the Novel Form itself. A Corrupted Patri- or Deus Alchemist retains their power with inverted purpose. The higher the Realm, the more catastrophic the transformation.
- The 7-level threat classification was designed partly around this escalation.

### ✅ Divine Domains
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- Over time, a god's accumulated Essence begins to organize spatially into a **Domain**: a region of the Void where the god's Essence has displaced ambient Chaos, creating a pocket of organized energy.
- Domains grow as gods grow — from simple protective boundaries into complex divine architecture expressing the god's nature.
- Gods shape their Domains deliberately. No two Domains are alike.

### ✅ Angels
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- Gods create **Angels** within their Domains: semi-conscious beings fashioned from the god's own Essence, given enough organization to act with purpose but not enough Verity to exist as truly independent entities.
- Angels serve as extensions of divine will — messengers, guardians, combatants in inter-divine conflict.
- Relatively cheap to produce. Benchmark: blessing one human costs the Essence equivalent of **33 Angels**.

### ✅ The 1 Blessed = 33 Angels Ratio
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- Blessing a single human requires 33 Angels' worth of Essence. Canonical and specific.
- The number 33 is symbolically chosen and will appear organically in religious texts and ancient doctrine — meant to feel discovered rather than invented.

### ✅ Prayer as Harvesting Infrastructure
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- When humans pray with genuine Verity directed at a specific god, the Stable Chaos flowing from the realm arrives at that god's Domain already **dedicated** — easier to absorb than generic ambient flow.
- A large, consistent congregation is a **harvesting beacon**. This is why gods build religions — not vanity, but harvesting infrastructure.
- The true cost of worship for gods: **identity**. Concentrated prayer carries worshippers' projections, slowly reshaping the god toward the image their congregation has constructed. Young gods are most vulnerable. Strong Verity and long experience allow resistance, but the pressure never stops.

### ✅ The Whisper — Full Mechanics
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- A Whisper is an intent-encoded Essence packet threaded from a god's Domain through the realm boundary to a specific human.
- Not language — compressed intent. The human's subconscious generates vision imagery to decode the received meaning. The god provides meaning; the human provides form. Two humans receiving identical intent will describe different visions.
- Timing: non-urgent Whispers surface during sleep; dwelling on them causes recurrence. Urgent Whispers break waking consciousness — more expensive.
- Whispering to an unlinked human costs meaningful Essence. Whispering through an established Blessed link costs a fraction and does not count toward the official allocation.

### ✅ Laws of Silence — Official Whisper Allocation and Reporting
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- Each god may issue 3 official Whispers per century to humans outside their Blessed network.
- Official Whispers must be reported to Paradox through a direct Essence message before or immediately after sending.
- Whispers to the Blessed are routine operation — cheap, unlimited, outside the allocation.

### ✅ Detection Ambiguity as Governance Tool
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- The metaphysical contract embedded in the Whisper technique allows Paradox to detect unofficial Whispers without the pantheon knowing with certainty that he can do so or how.
- Gods suspect detection capability exists but cannot map the specific mechanism.
- Compliance = self-interest (his protection is worth the constraint) + unquantifiable detection risk. The ambiguity is a deliberate governance tool — more effective than either factor alone.

### ✅ The Blessed — Full Mechanics
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- Blessed receive: enhanced cultivation (accelerated realm progression), strengthened Verity, The Warmth (physical sensation of divine attention), and access to Divine Intervention in crises.
- Gods gain: perception (observation windows into the realm) and influence (instruments for building religious infrastructure). Not Essence — the Blessed does not feed the god.
- The Divine Eye is finite and active, not omniscient. Gods must choose where to look.
- Paradox provides no Divine Interventions to his Blessed. His network model is observation, not active management.

### ✅ Paradox's Blessed Network
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- Paradox maintains more Blessed than any other god. Three compounding reasons: (1) his Blessed live up to two centuries due to enhanced cultivation extending lifespan; (2) his Essence reserves absorb the 33-Angels cost more easily; (3) his per-Blessed investment is shallow — no interventions, rare Whispers, observation-window model. Width made possible by depth not spent.
- He has been accumulating Blessed for 12,000 years. Mass worship ≠ more Blessed.

### ✅ Blessed Death — Harvest and Recycling
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- At a Blessed's death, accumulated Essence returns partially to the god through the link. Higher cultivation realm = more substantial harvest.
- If the recovered Essence meets the 33 Angels threshold, the god can use it to bless another human directly — bypassing the realm-barrier threading cost.
- Below the threshold: absorbed into reserves. Creates a compounding dynamic for gods with high-realm Blessed networks.

### ✅ Hereditary Blessed Lineages
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- Blessing a pregnant woman transfers the divine investment to the child at birth.
- Efficient in theory (no second Covenant cost); risky in practice (cannot assess the child's temperament).
- ❓ Unresolved: whether a hereditary Blessed has truly entered a Covenant, having never consciously recognized or acknowledged the divine Essence.

### ✅ Unproductive Blessed — Accepted Variance
**Canonical as of:** 2026-05-19

- Blessed who build nothing are an accepted loss in a long-term strategy.
- For gods focused on harvesting infrastructure, an unproductive Blessed is a pure loss.
- For Paradox, the observation window has value regardless — he counts it as worthwhile even when no congregation forms.
